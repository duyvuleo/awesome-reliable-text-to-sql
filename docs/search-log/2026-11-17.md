Its [note](../../papers/notes/luthorsql.md) distinguishes confidence-based routing from calibrated answerability, candidate handoff from question-only API routing, and author-recorded registrations from externally timestamped preregistration. Some configurations lose accuracy or increase cost. Development-set reuse, unequal execution limits and package/scoring differences constrain generalization and reproduction.
