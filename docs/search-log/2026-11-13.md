## Added after full-paper reading

[NL2SQL is a solved problem... Not!](https://vldb.org/cidrdb/2024/nl2sql-is-a-solved-problem-not.html), a CIDR 2024 position paper, fills a historical gap directly within the collection's scope. Its eight pages and all four figures were read. The [detailed note](../../papers/notes/nl2sql-not-solved.md) separates annotator agreement from detection accuracy, an unrelated-database mismatch experiment from general answerability, and relaxed execution matching from semantic equivalence. Publication identity was also checked against [an author's publication page](https://zhaofuheng.github.io/).

The paper's reference to [Archerfish](https://github.com/archerfish-bench/benchmark) led to inspection of its public driver, adapter and comparator inventory, README and Apache-2.0 license. At [the inspected revision](https://github.com/archerfish-bench/benchmark/tree/620fbcc4cff11dfbc8d17db20fc762073b43afa3), the default relaxed comparator ignores row order, conditionally removes duplicates, rounds numbers and treats None and zero as matching. Its documentation does not exactly describe numeric rounding or string-value case handling. The tool entry preserves these evaluator limits; the source was read, not executed.

[The Case for Intent-Based Query Rewriting](https://www.vldb.org/2025/Workshops/VLDB-Workshops-2025/DATAI/DATAI25_5.pdf), DATAI 2025, was read in full, including figures and its ten-query human study. It is included as adjacent work on reformulation over available sources when original tables cannot be accessed. The [reading note](../../papers/notes/inqure.md) distinguishes related analytical alternatives from equivalent answers. Its evaluation uses table schemas and human plausibility judgments, without validating results or speedups over populated tables. Schema-level executability checks do exist.

The official [InQuRe implementation](https://github.com/dbislab/InQuRe/tree/61c48e36a7cd5cf81e7cd56257ef9631e572a861) is recorded separately as a system. Its main program rejects natural-language inputs, its user-study data are not uploaded, and repository licensing remains unverified. Neither the existing Spider schemas nor unreleased human judgments are counted as a new dataset.

[Tracing Recurrent and Emergent Hallucination Patterns in Decomposed Text-to-SQL Generation](https://aclanthology.org/2026.uncertainlp-main.40/), UncertaiNLP 2026, adds an adjacent diagnostic study. All 11 pages, appendices and visual results were reviewed. The [note](../../papers/notes/drilldown-hallucinations.md) distinguishes category-level conditional recurrence from causal error propagation and structural heuristics from semantic labels. Its [public diagnostic artifacts](https://github.com/HaydenMM/bird-mini-drilldown/tree/9050803cada1a0750d783685a2508c95fc45e3b0) include source, saved generations and annotations; these are recorded as one evaluation tool, not another independent BIRD dataset. Licensing and independent reproduction remain unverified.
