The [code documentation](https://github.com/RishiiGamer2201/counterfactual-sql-rewards/blob/d38373a4a7140bcca9890066c491e9205d9944f0/README.md) and [dataset release](https://huggingface.co/datasets/counterfactual-sql/counterfactual-sql-data/tree/8bf2b42a1578534a2dbd630b5bb12479bc0f12df) were checked separately. Correctness remains relative to a supplied reference.
- [Sports Agent](../../papers/notes/sports-agent.md) reports partial answers with explicit gaps when retrieval covers only part of a request. Its BIRD-Interact experiments measure simulated task reward. The evidence for the partial-answer policy comes from a separate, bundled prompt change. We added the paper without counting the existing benchmark again.
