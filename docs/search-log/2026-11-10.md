## Additions after full-paper reading

Four papers were added, each with a complete, version-specific [reading note](../../papers/reading-notes.md). This batch includes older coverage gaps as well as current proceedings and preprint material.

- [Concept based Ambiguity Resolution in LLMs](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v328/hu26b.html): read the full 17-page CPAL 2026 paper and appendices. Its AMBROSIA-derived detection experiment establishes direct relevance, while API-call retrieval is a separate task. Generated interpretations serve as unambiguous controls; the threshold protocol and absence of SQL/clarification outcome evaluation are qualified. The differently titled [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.11679) is the same work.
- [Node-Level Uncertainty Estimation in LLM-Generated SQL](https://aclanthology.org/2026.lrec-1.168/): read all nine LREC pages and Appendix A. Added as adjacent confidence/error-localization work. Labels omit missing clauses, and reported ROC-AUC measures discrimination rather than demonstrated probability calibration. The [2025 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.13984) is retained as lineage, not a second entry.
- [SQLoom](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03298): read all 12 pages of v1, including Appendices A–F. Included as adjacent work for counterexample-database supervision and selected Spider annotation repairs. A separating database witnesses query disagreement, while intended-meaning judgments still depend on references and model checks. The [author page](https://people.cs.vt.edu/penggao/publication.html) lists a preprint; a future-venue template header does not establish acceptance.
- [OmniTQA](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-aacl.200/): resolved the earlier lead by reading all 24 proceedings pages and appendices. Alternative schema mappings and vague predicates are explored through hybrid SQL/LLM plans, but the system selects an answer without user clarification or abstention evaluation. Oracle ACC@6 and reference-free answer selection are distinguished. The [earlier preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02444v2) has a different title and shorter body; it was used for identity reconciliation only.
