## Added after complete reading

[Two Surfaces of Ambiguity](https://vldb.org/2026/Workshops/VLDB-Workshops-2026/AIDB/aidb26_10.pdf) was read through all ten pages, figures and tables. The [reading note](../../papers/notes/two-surfaces.md) separates improved detection coverage from downstream success: the union does not significantly outperform introspection alone under BIRD-Interact's stress budget. AMBROSIA AllFound gains come with lower precision and more candidates. The three-call headline counts generation only; simulator encoding and subsequent agent calls are additional. Multi-label replay changes annotation credit while retaining the primary label supplied to the decoder.
